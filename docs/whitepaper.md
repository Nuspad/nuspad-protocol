# Nuspad Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** A Solana where anyone can read market momentum in seconds, without hopping between a dozen tabs.

**Mission.** Put the most active tokens on one fast, honest board. Show what is confirmed, label what is not, and never dress a guess up as a fact.

Principles:

1. **Clarity first.** One page, one ranking, readable on a phone.
2. **Honest data.** Figures come straight from an open market feed. When the feed is silent, the page says so.
3. **Quiet by default.** No polling while nobody is looking. No motion for people who ask for none.
